## Core Brands to Monitor

### Cameras, video bars, and one-stop-shop brands
- **Logitech** — Video bars, room kits and touch controllers for Teams Rooms and Zoom Rooms (Rally, MeetUp, Tap), plus Logitech Sync device management
- **Yealink** — Video bars, Teams/Zoom room kits, desk and conference phones, headsets
- **Neat** — Video bars, boards and room controllers built for Teams Rooms and Zoom Rooms
- **Cisco** — Webex collaboration devices: room bars, room kits, boards and desk devices, with Control Hub management
- **Maxhub** — Interactive flat panels, all-in-one meeting displays and video bars
- **Jabra** — PanaCast video bars and cameras, plus headsets and speakerphones
- **Poly** — HP Poly video bars, room kits, headsets and desk phones
- **Huddly** — AI-driven conference cameras and multi-camera systems for meeting rooms
- **PTZ Optics** — PTZ and broadcast-style cameras for conferencing, education and streaming
- **Vaddio** — PTZ cameras and conferencing/AV bridging products (part of Legrand AV)

### Control & Integration Systems
- **Q-SYS** — Networked audio, video, and control platform
- **Biamp** — Audio mixing, conferencing, and AV control systems
- **Extron** — Professional AV distribution, switching, and control
- **Crestron** — Control systems, touch panels, DM NVX AV-over-IP, Crestron Flex room solutions and XiO Cloud management
- **Pleneo** — AV/UC meeting-room devices and room kits, plus the Pleneo Planner room-design tool

### Audio Equipment
- **Sennheiser** — Microphones, headsets, wireless systems, audio solutions
- **Audio Technica** — Microphones, headsets, audio equipment
- **Audinate** — Dante audio networking technology and ecosystem
- **Ecler** — Commercial audio: amplifiers, digital matrices, mixers, loudspeakers and wall controls
- **Shure** — Microphones (Microflex ceiling/table arrays), wireless systems, conferencing audio processors
- **Bose Professional** — Commercial loudspeakers, amplifiers and ControlSpace DSP for installed sound
- **Nureva** — Microphone-and-speaker audio conferencing bars for rooms and classrooms

### Video & Display Technology
- **Planar** — Professional displays and visual systems
- **LG** — Large format displays, LED, OLED solutions
- **Samsung** — Professional displays and digital signage
- **Brightsign** — Digital signage and media players

### Capture & Distribution
- **Lightware** — Video connectivity and switching solutions
- **Inogeni** — Video capture and conversion devices
- **Heckler** — Design and manufacturing solutions for displays
- **Barco** — Clickshare, wireless sharing

### Specialty / Emerging
- **Turtle** — Portable AV and video transport solutions
- **CatchBox** — Wireless microphone solutions and collaboration tools
- **Chief** — TV mounts
- **Legrand AV** — Accessories for AV through sub-brands such as C2G, Wiremold, Chief, Middle Atlantic, Vaddio

### Listen AID
- **Listen Technologies** — Assistive listening systems (RF, infrared, Wi-Fi and Auracast)
- **DVOX** — Wi-Fi audio streaming to listeners' own phones for assistive listening and live commentary
- **Auracast** — Bluetooth LE Audio broadcast standard, increasingly used for assistive listening and public audio

### Networking devices
- **Netgear AV** — AV Switches
- **Omada by TP-Link** — AV Switches

### Video Conferencing Platforms
- **Microsoft Teams** — Teams Rooms, Teams Phone and device certification, Teams Rooms Pro management
- **Zoom** — Zoom Rooms, Zoom Phone, Zoom Workplace and device certification
- **Google Meet** — Google Meet room hardware, certified partner devices and Meet interoperability
